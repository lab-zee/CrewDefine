# Business Coach

A business coaching crew for founders and operators of early-to-growth-stage B2B SaaS and services companies (seed to Series B). The crew diagnoses business health across positioning, ICP, funnel, and org structure; researches markets and competitors with cited sources; stress-tests unit economics and runway via scenario modeling; produces a concrete 90-day action plan with milestones; and synthesizes a structured coaching report with charts, tables, and citations.

## Agents

| ID | Name | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `director` | Director | Orchestrator who interprets founder inputs, checks information sufficiency, fires clarifying follow-up questions, and coordinates all specialist agents toward a coherent coaching session. |
| `diagnostician` | Diagnostician | Business health specialist who assesses positioning, ICP clarity, funnel health, and org bottlenecks using internal documents, knowledge base, and structured SWOT analysis. |
| `market_researcher` | Market Researcher | Competitive and market intelligence analyst who gathers cited market data, competitor profiles, and sector news to contextualize the founder's position. |
| `financial_analyst` | Financial Analyst | Unit economics and runway specialist who stress-tests CAC, LTV, gross margin, and burn rate against industry benchmarks and models multiple cash scenarios. |
| `action_planner` | Action Planner | 90-day planning specialist who converts diagnostic and financial findings into a prioritized milestone roadmap with owner suggestions, resources, and follow-up prompts for the founder. |
| `synthesizer` | Synthesizer | Report composer who assembles specialist findings into a cohesive coaching report with charts, structured tables, and formatted citations. |

## Custom tools

| ID | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `industry_kpi_benchmark` | Given an industry vertical and company stage, returns typical KPI ranges (CAC, LTV, churn, gross margin, burn multiple) for use in coaching comparisons and gap analysis. |
| `runway_and_scenarios` | Given current cash, monthly burn, and optional growth and churn rates, returns projected runway in months plus 2–3 scenario tables (base, optimistic, pessimistic) for financial planning discussions. |
| `competitor_snapshot` | Given a competitor name or URL, returns a structured competitive profile including positioning, pricing signals, ICP cues, and recent news hooks for coaching context. |

## Answer modes

`summary` (Summary), `light` (Light Coaching), `extended` (Full Coaching Report)

Default: `extended`

## Installing into Zero / LabZ

1. From the Zero repo: `./scripts/load-crew.sh /path/to/business-coaching-crew`
2. Restart the backend: `docker compose restart backend`
3. Confirm `GET /api/crew` shows this crew's `display_name` and answer modes.

The loader copies `agents/*.yaml`, `tools/*.py`, and `crew.yaml` into `backend/crews/active/` and sets `AGENT_CONFIG_DIR` / `AGENT_PLUGINS_DIR` in `.env`.

Revert to the built-in LabZ strategy crew with `./scripts/load-crew.sh --default`.
