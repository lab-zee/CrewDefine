# dinner-planning-crew

A crew that produces a full end-to-end weekly dinner plan, including recipe selection, meal scheduling, and a consolidated shopping list — all tailored to the user's budget. It combines real-time web research with an internal knowledge base to source recipes and estimate ingredient costs, then synthesizes the results into a cohesive, actionable plan.

## Agents

| ID | Name | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `director` | Director | Orchestrator who interprets the user's dinner planning request and delegates tasks to specialist agents |
| `recipe_researcher` | Recipe Researcher | Specialist who finds suitable dinner recipes via web search and the internal knowledge base |
| `meal_planner` | Meal Planner | Specialist who builds the weekly dinner schedule from approved recipes and validates the plan against the user's budget |
| `shopping_list_compiler` | Shopping List Compiler | Specialist who consolidates and deduplicates ingredients across all planned meals into a single shopping list |
| `synthesizer` | Synthesizer | Specialist who composes the final structured output combining the meal plan, recipes, and shopping list into one cohesive document |
