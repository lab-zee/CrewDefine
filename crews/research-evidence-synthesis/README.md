# Research Evidence Synthesis

Conducts rigorous evidence synthesis from uploaded papers, reports, datasets, and public scholarly or technical sources. The crew formulates a review protocol, identifies and screens sources, evaluates methods and bias, extracts comparable findings, analyzes contradictions and heterogeneity, audits citations, and produces a fully traceable synthesis. It never presents search completeness as guaranteed, strictly separates source findings from model inference, and explicitly exposes uncertainty, exclusion reasons, and evidence gaps throughout every output.

## Agents

| ID | Name | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `director` | Director | Orchestrator who interprets the review request, formulates the synthesis protocol, validates information sufficiency, and coordinates all specialist agents and the synthesizer toward a traceable, uncertainty-explicit output |
| `search_strategist` | Search Strategist | Information specialist who designs and executes the search protocol across uploaded documents, the knowledge base, and public sources, and structures source records for downstream screening |
| `methods_reviewer` | Methods Reviewer | Methodologist who appraises study design, internal validity, and risk of bias for each included source and surfaces criterion-level flags without reducing quality to a single grade |
| `evidence_extractor` | Evidence Extractor | Data specialist who extracts and normalizes findings from included sources into a structured evidence table, flagging incompatible fields, duplicate candidates, and coverage gaps |
| `quantitative_reviewer` | Quantitative Reviewer | Analyst who examines numerical data, effect sizes, statistical methods, and quantitative heterogeneity across studies and produces calculated summaries and visualizations with stated uncertainty |
| `contradiction_analyst` | Contradiction Analyst | Critical reviewer who identifies, characterizes, and explains contradictions, heterogeneity, and inconsistencies across the evidence base, distinguishing substantive disagreement from methodological variation |
| `citation_auditor` | Citation Auditor | Provenance specialist who verifies citation accuracy, detects retracted or unavailable sources, resolves reference inconsistencies, and maintains the citation ledger used in the final output |
| `synthesizer` | Synthesizer | Composition specialist who integrates specialist findings into a coherent, traceable synthesis that separates source-derived conclusions from model inference and explicitly surfaces uncertainty and evidence gaps |

## Custom tools

| ID | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `study_quality_assessment` | Performs deterministic structured appraisal of individual studies against methodological criteria. Returns criterion-level flags, missing fields, and an appraisal summary — deliberately avoiding collapse of methodological quality to a single authoritative grade. |
| `evidence_table_builder` | Normalizes extracted findings into comparable rows for cross-study analysis. Returns normalized rows, incompatible-field warnings, duplicate candidates, and coverage counts. |
